Kerla / Wedding photographer

Wedding photographer in Hastings, East Sussex & across the UK

We photograph and film weddings across East Sussex and the rest of the UK, from country-house celebrations to traditional Nigerian ceremonies full of colour, music and family. We stay close to the story of the day and keep the light and colour true. Based in Hastings, we regularly shoot in Eastbourne, Brighton and Tunbridge Wells, and travel anywhere in the UK.

What we cover: Bridal preparations, ceremony and traditional rites, couple portraits, family groups, reception and first dance, and cinematic wedding films.
