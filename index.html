The studio

Small team, big days.

Kerla is a creative media studio based in Hastings, East Sussex. We regularly shoot in Eastbourne, Brighton and Tunbridge Wells, and travel anywhere in the UK and abroad. We photograph and film people at the moments that matter to them, from traditional ceremonies and country-house weddings to studio sittings and family milestones. Colour is rich but true, light is shaped rather than flattened, and every frame gets the time it needs in the edit.

  • Weddings & traditional ceremonies
  • Portraits & headshots
  • Family & milestones
  • Corporate & commercial
  • Events
  • Cinematic film
